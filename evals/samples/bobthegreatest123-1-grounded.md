- Evidence: [[G4 13...Bxf6]] left your queen on c7 to be taken by Bxc7. In [[G5 13.Nf3]] you left your bishop on f4 undefended, and Bxf4+ won it. [[G3 11.Qxe5+]] put your queen on e5 where dxe5 captured it.

- Habit: Before you release the move, name the piece you just moved and every other piece that is undefended. Then ask "can they capture something?"
