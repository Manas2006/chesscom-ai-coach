## Patterns to fix

**1. Missing the opponent's threat before moving (3 of 3 losses)**
- Evidence: [[G4 12...f5]] opened the e-pawn, so [[G4 13.exf6]] uncovered Bf4 against your queen on c7, and you lost the queen on [[G4 14.Bxc7]]. In G2, [[G2 10...Qf5]] ignored the threat to f7, and [[G2 11.Nxf7]] forked both rooks. In G3, [[G3 15.g4]] left c4 attacked twice (Be6 and Ne5) and defended once, and you ended a pawn down.
- Why: In each case your move served your own plan (a pawn push, a queen sortie, a kingside advance) while the opponent's last move created a threat. Your own pawn on e5 in G4 was hiding a pin-like line, which is easy to forget.
- Habit: Before every move ask "What does their last move attack, and what does my move unblock?" Check checks, captures and threats against your queen and rooks.
